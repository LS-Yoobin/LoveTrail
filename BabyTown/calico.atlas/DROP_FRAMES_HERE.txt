Cat animation frames for Artemis (calico skin)

Drop PNG files here (transparent background).

Keep each frame at most 512×512 px (longest side) so Xcode can pack the atlas
under the 2048 GPU limit. Larger source art will fail the build.

Names must match exactly:

  idle_0.png, idle_1.png, ...
  walk_0.png, walk_1.png, ...
  sit_0.png, sit_1.png, ...
  sleep_0.png, ...
  groom_0.png, ...
  eat_0.png, ...
  pounce_0.png, ...
  petReaction_0.png, ...

After adding files: build the app in Xcode (Product → Build).

This folder is a SpriteKit texture atlas. You do NOT add these to Assets.xcassets.
