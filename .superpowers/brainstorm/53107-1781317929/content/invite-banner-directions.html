A — Frosted backdrop

Add a blurred material behind the entire bottom chrome. Visually lifts the chrome off the scroll layer. Banner card keeps its current light-blue fill, but the blur creates clear separation between the scrolling content and the actions below.

B — Warm cream / amber

Swap the banner fill to a warm cream with a soft amber border. Hue is completely different from the cool blue scroll cards, so it immediately reads as a separate element. Works without any blur — pure colour contrast.

C — Accent CTA

Lean into the accent blue — light blue-tinted fill, accent border, left accent stripe, and a small pill badge ("Send invite"). Makes the banner feel like a call-to-action in the app's primary colour, clearly distinct from neutral content cards.

These can also be combined — e.g. A+B (blur backdrop + warm banner) or A+C (blur + accent CTA). Click to select then let me know.
