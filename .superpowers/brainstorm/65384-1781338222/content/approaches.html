Implementation approach

Three ways to build the zoom animation + paginated capture reveal

APPROACH A — Inline animation on welcome step
WelcomeStep holds all animation state:
• bookScale: 1.0 → 8.0
• bookOffset: (0,0) → (-screenW/4, 0)
• capturesOpacity: 0 → 1
Book image scales + pans right page into
full screen, then captures fade in on top.
One "step" handles everything.

All in welcome step

Simplest from a navigation standpoint — no new step enum cases. Welcome step grows to own the zoom + reveal. Downside: the step becomes complex.

APPROACH B — New GiftRevealStep (recommended)
Step enum: .welcome → .giftReveal → .username
WelcomeStep stays simple — tapping
"Open it" just calls advance().
GiftRevealStep owns the animation:
• .onAppear: zoom + pan book image
• then: fade in capture content
• Prev / Next pagination
• "Open our space" → advance()

Separate GiftRevealStep

Clean separation. WelcomeStep keeps its crossfade-out, GiftRevealStep owns the dramatic zoom. Easy to iterate on animation without touching welcome. My recommendation.

APPROACH C — fullScreenCover overlay
Welcome presents a .fullScreenCover
that starts transparent and contains
a copy of the BookFlipView already
matched to the welcome book's position.
• Hero-style matched geometry
• Seamless positional continuity
• Complex coordinate math
• Harder to debug

Hero transition

Most cinematic — the book image appears to travel continuously from its welcome position into the full-screen reveal. Much harder to get right in SwiftUI.
