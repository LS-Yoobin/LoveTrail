Where does the gift reveal fit?

When "Open it" is tapped, the book zooms in and captures appear — but when in the flow does this happen?

OPTION A — Gift first
1. Welcome screen (book animating)
→ tap "Open it"
2. 🎬 Book zooms → captures reveal
3. "What should we call you?" (username)
4. Email
5. Profile photo
6. Color theme
7. Home

Gift reveal immediately

The animation plays right when "Open it" is tapped. Partner sees the gift first, then fills in their profile. More emotional — the gift is the hook.

OPTION B — Gift last (current plan)
1. Welcome screen (book animating)
→ tap "Open it"
2. "What should we call you?" (username)
3. Email
4. Profile photo
5. Color theme
6. 🎬 Book zooms → captures reveal
7. Home

Gift reveal at the end

Profile setup happens first, then the animated reveal closes out onboarding. Captures feel like a reward after they've committed to the app.
