Covela · app map for the design team · Jul 2026

Two lines, one map.

Everything in Covela is the story of two people on two phones. The rose line is the person who starts the space; the blue line is the one who joins. They ride alone through onboarding, meet at the pairing moment, and travel together through the Cove. The two colors aren’t decoration: they are the app’s two real themes, Pink and Blue, and every screen you design must work on both lines.

Under the hood · For when engineering talks

Strict MVVM, four floors down

Every screen you design maps onto this stack. The rule that never bends: views never talk to services.

View

SwiftUI screens and components. Renders state, calls ViewModel methods, never touches a Service.

Views/ Components/
ViewModel

@MainActor classes owning @Published state — one per feature.

HomeViewModel PreludeViewModel PetViewModel WatchTogetherViewModel
Service

Singletons (.shared) doing the real work — persistence, networking, engines.

DataPersistenceManager AuthService ThemeManager NotificationManager StoreManager
Model

Plain Codable structs. No side effects, ever.

Moment PreludeCapture Pet LetterBlock ArchiveBundle

On device — privacy first

Photos, notes, and voice memos live in the Documents directory and UserDefaults. Auth tokens sit in the Keychain. Nothing leaves unless the user pairs or shares.

DataPersistenceManager KeychainTokenStore LocalPolaroidStore

Backend — MongoDB

Pairing, gift sync, archives, and call signaling flow through a small family of API clients.

Engines

SpriteKit renders the pet room. WebRTC carries watch-together calls. AVFoundation plays voice memos, the gift song, and background music.
